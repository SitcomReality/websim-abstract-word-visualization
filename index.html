Esoteric Sphere: Philosophical Physics

How to Play

🔮

Philosophical Spheres

Each sphere represents a philosophical concept with unique properties and effects.

Click on spheres to activate them and harvest energy.

⚡

Resonance Chains

Activate compatible concepts in sequence to create powerful resonance chains.

Chains multiply energy output and create special field effects.

🧪

Concept Fusion

Drag compatible spheres together to fuse them into new philosophical concepts.

Fusion costs 15 energy but can yield powerful new spheres or large energy rewards.

⚙️

Upgrades

Spend energy in the shop to permanently enhance your spheres and abilities.

Strategic upgrades will help you generate energy more efficiently.
